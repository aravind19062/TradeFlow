# Derivatives and leverage: the evidence

## Most individual traders in equity F&O lose money

SEBI studies of individual traders in the equity futures and options segment have repeatedly found that a
large majority, roughly nine in ten in the studies published in recent years, made net losses, and that
transaction costs added substantially to those losses. Check SEBI's website for the latest figures.

## Why options are especially risky for beginners

* **Time decay:** an option loses value as expiry approaches, all else equal.
* **Non-linear pay-offs:** small moves in the underlying share can cause large percentage moves in the
  option.
* **Selling options** can expose you to losses far larger than the premium received.

## TradeFlow's scope

TradeFlow does not offer leveraged products, margin trading or derivatives in either demo or live
trading. This module exists so that you recognise these risks if you encounter them elsewhere.

> **Key idea:** leverage does not change whether a trade idea is good. It changes how fast a bad one can
> ruin you.
