# Operational mistakes

Some losses come from how an order is entered rather than from the market.

* **Fat-finger errors:** typing 1000 instead of 100, or a price with a misplaced decimal. Always read the
  confirmation screen. TradeFlow's live trading requires an explicit review step for every order.
* **Wrong side:** selling when you meant to buy. Check the colour and wording of the action.
* **Wrong instrument:** similar names or symbols. Check the exchange and the full name.
* **Market orders in illiquid shares:** a wide spread or thin book can give a very poor fill.
* **Forgotten open orders:** an old limit order fills unexpectedly days later (if it has a long
  validity). Review open orders regularly.
* **Not knowing the product type:** intraday orders may be squared off automatically at the end of
  the day.

## Security

* Never share your broker password, PIN or one-time codes, including with support staff or "advisers".
* Use two-factor authentication everywhere it is offered. TradeFlow requires it for live trading.
* Review your broker's contract notes and statements. Report anything you did not authorise immediately.

## When things go wrong

If you are unsure what happened to an order, check its status with your broker before placing another.
Duplicate orders are a common way to double a mistake.
