# Behavioural traps

Most trading mistakes are not about missing information. They are predictable human reactions.

| Trap | What it looks like | A defence |
|---|---|---|
| **Overconfidence** | A few wins and you increase size sharply | Fixed position-sizing rules |
| **Loss aversion** | Holding losers too long, selling winners too early | Pre-set exits; judge positions on today's merits |
| **Revenge trading** | Trading immediately after a loss to "win it back" | A daily loss limit; stop and review |
| **FOMO** | Buying after a big rally because everyone is talking about it | A written plan required before every entry |
| **Confirmation bias** | Only reading opinions that agree with you | Write down what would prove you wrong |
| **Anchoring** | "It was ₹800 last month, so ₹500 is cheap" | Value the business today, not the old price |
| **Overtrading** | Many small trades out of boredom or excitement | Track costs; limit trades per day |

## Tips and social media

Messages promising guaranteed returns, "sure-shot" tips, or paid groups that trade on your behalf are red
flags. Many are scams. Registered investment advisers and research analysts are regulated and must
disclose who they are.

## Gamification

Streaks, confetti and leaderboards encourage trading more often, not better. TradeFlow deliberately avoids
them, and never uses trade counts or demo profits to judge readiness.
