# Measuring performance honestly

## Simple return

`(end value − start value) / start value`, adjusted for any money you added or withdrew in between.

## Drawdown

A **drawdown** is a fall from a previous peak. **Maximum drawdown** is the largest such fall over a
period. A portfolio that rose 30% but fell 25% along the way was much harder to hold than its final
return suggests.

## Compare like with like

* Compare against a sensible benchmark, such as a broad index fund, not against your best-performing
  friend.
* Include all costs and taxes.
* Use enough time. A few weeks of results mostly measure luck.

## Survivorship and hindsight

It is easy to remember the winners and forget the losers, and to believe in hindsight that an outcome was
predictable. Keeping a written record of why you made each trade, before you know the result, is one of
the few reliable defences.

## Demo results are not evidence of skill

Demo trading uses virtual money and simulated prices. Demo profits do not show that you would profit with
real money, and TradeFlow never uses demo profits to decide whether you are ready for live trading.
