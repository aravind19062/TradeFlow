# Making a trading plan before you trade

Decisions made in advance, while calm, tend to be better than decisions made while watching prices move.
A plan for each trade answers:

1. **Why** am I entering? What would show I am wrong?
2. **Where** is my exit if I am wrong (the invalidation level, often expressed as a stop)?
3. **How much** am I prepared to lose on this trade, in rupees? (See the position-sizing module.)
4. **How large** is the position, given that loss limit?
5. **What** will I do if the price gaps through my exit?

## Limits for the whole account

* A maximum loss per trade (many risk frameworks use a small fixed fraction of the account).
* A maximum loss per day or week, after which you stop trading and review.
* A maximum number of open positions and a maximum weight per position.

## Review without hindsight

Record each plan before the trade. Afterwards, ask whether you **followed** the plan, not only whether the
trade made money. A good decision can lose, and a bad decision can win.
