# Understanding risk

## Risk is the possibility of loss

Every trade can lose money. Risk management does not avoid losses. It keeps any single loss, or a run of
losses, from doing damage you cannot recover from.

## The arithmetic of losses

Losses and gains are not symmetric:

| Loss | Gain needed to get back to where you started |
|---|---|
| 10% | 11.1% |
| 25% | 33.3% |
| 50% | 100% |
| 75% | 300% |

Protecting capital matters more than chasing gains.

## Kinds of risk

* **Market risk:** the whole market falls.
* **Company-specific risk:** one company disappoints.
* **Liquidity risk:** you cannot exit at a fair price.
* **Gap risk:** the price jumps past your exit level, for example overnight.
* **Leverage risk:** borrowed money magnifies losses (see the leverage module).
* **Operational risk:** wrong order entry, technology failure, a lost connection.
* **Behavioural risk:** your own reactions under stress.

## Only risk money you can afford to lose

Money you will need for living costs, debt repayments, emergencies or near-term goals should not be in
short-term trading.
