# Realized and unrealized profit and loss

## Unrealized P&L

`(current price − average cost) × quantity held`

It is a **paper** gain or loss. It changes with every price move and becomes real only when you sell.

## Realized P&L

When you sell, the difference between the sale price and the average cost of the shares sold becomes
**realized**:

`(sale price − average cost) × quantity sold`

Example: you hold 20 shares at an average cost of ₹110 and sell 5 at ₹130. Realized P&L is
(130 − 110) × 5 = **₹100**. The remaining 15 shares keep the ₹110 average cost.

## Total return

Total P&L = realized + unrealized (before costs and taxes). Return is usually expressed against what you
put in. The demo uses your virtual deposit.

## A common trap

Holding losers to avoid "making the loss real" does not change the economic loss. The money is already
lost on paper; the question is only whether the position still makes sense today.

> **Key idea:** a loss is a loss whether or not you sell. Decide on the merits of the position, not on
> whether a loss has been "booked".
