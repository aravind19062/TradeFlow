# Holdings and positions

Brokers usually show two different views of what you own.

## Holdings

**Holdings** are shares that have been settled into your demat account, typically from purchases on
earlier days. They are investments you own outright, valued at the current market price.

## Positions

**Positions** are today's open trades and any intraday or derivative exposure that has not been settled
into holdings. A share bought today usually appears under positions until settlement moves it to
holdings.

## Average cost

If you buy 10 shares at ₹100 and then 10 more at ₹120, your **average cost** is
(10 × 100 + 10 × 120) / 20 = **₹110** per share. Selling part of the holding does not change the average
cost of the shares you keep.

## Long and short

* **Long:** you own the shares and benefit if the price rises.
* **Short:** you have sold shares you do not own, expecting to buy them back cheaper. Short selling has
  additional rules and, in theory, unlimited loss. TradeFlow's demo is long-only.
