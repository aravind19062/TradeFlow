# Live trading risk acknowledgement (version 1)

Before TradeFlow lets you request live-trading access, please read and confirm each statement.

1. **I can lose money.** Live trading uses real money held in my own broker account. Prices can fall
   quickly and gap past any stop-loss I set. I can lose some or all of the money I trade with.
2. **Only money I can afford to lose.** I will not trade with money I need for living costs, debt
   repayments, emergencies or near-term goals, and I will not borrow to trade.
3. **This is not advice or a judgement of suitability.** Completing the Trading Academy and passing the
   readiness assessment show that I know some core concepts. They do not mean trading is suitable for me,
   do not guarantee competence or profits, and are not investment advice. My decisions are my own.
4. **Demo results prove nothing.** Demo trading uses simulated prices and virtual money. Demo profits do not
   show that I would profit with real money, and TradeFlow did not use them to assess me.
5. **My broker decides too.** My broker carries out its own KYC and eligibility checks and its own risk
   controls. Passing TradeFlow's assessment does not replace them, and the broker may refuse or restrict
   my account or orders.
6. **I am responsible for every order.** I will check each order's instrument, side, quantity, type and
   price before confirming it. Orders that execute cannot be undone.
7. **TradeFlow can restrict access.** TradeFlow may pause or revoke live-trading access (for example after a
   policy change, after the qualification expires, or for my protection). I can also exclude myself from live
   trading for a period at any time.
